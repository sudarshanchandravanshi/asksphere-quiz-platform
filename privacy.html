Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

AskSphere lets people create and play friendship and personality quizzes. There are no user accounts in this version.

Information we store

  • Quiz content: When a friendship quiz is created, its title, creator name, questions, answer choices and correct answers are stored so the share link can work and results can be calculated. Personality quiz content is carried in its share link.
  • Public leaderboard: A player’s nickname and score are stored and displayed on that quiz’s public leaderboard only if the player checks the explicit sharing consent box. Do not use your real name if you do not want it public.
  • Answers: Individual player answers are processed to calculate the result and are not saved as a response history.
  • Technical data: The hosting and storage providers may process technical information such as request metadata and logs to operate and secure the service.

Storage and retention

Quiz data and opted-in leaderboard entries are stored using Vercel Blob. This version does not provide a self-service deletion feature or an automatic retention period; records may remain available until the site operator deletes them. Share links should be treated as public to anyone who receives them.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising is not guaranteed to be active. If Google AdSense or other advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies. Where required, consent choices will be presented before non-essential cookies or personalized advertising are used. The site operator must configure the appropriate consent platform and regional disclosures before serving ads.

Third-party providers

AskSphere is hosted on Vercel and uses Vercel Blob for quiz and leaderboard storage. Their services process information as needed to provide hosting and storage, under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Your choices and requests

Do not submit sensitive personal information in quizzes. To request access to or deletion of quiz or leaderboard data, contact the site operator using the support contact published on the Contact page. A monitored support email and a documented deletion process must be configured by the operator before public launch.

Policy updates

This policy must be reviewed and updated if analytics, advertising, account features, storage providers or data practices change. It is general product information, not legal advice.
