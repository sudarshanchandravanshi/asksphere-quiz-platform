GOOD TO KNOW

AskSphere FAQs

Helpful answers before you create a quiz.

Is AskSphere free?

Yes. Creating and playing quizzes is free in this version. Advertising is not currently configured.

Can I write my own questions?

Yes. Start from scratch or choose from 15 friendship prompts and 15 personality prompts. Edit questions, four answer choices and result types.

How do friendship scores work?

The creator selects one correct answer per question. Players earn one point for each correct choice. New friendship quiz links keep answer keys on the server.

How do personality results work?

Each answer maps to a result type. The most frequently selected type wins. If counts tie, the first result type in your list is shown.

Are my answers public?

Individual answers are processed to calculate a result and are not shown on the public leaderboard. A nickname and friendship score are published only after the player opts in.

Can I delete a quiz?

Self-service deletion is not available yet. Avoid including private details. A monitored support contact and deletion process still need to be configured by the site operator.
