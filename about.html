About AskSphere

AskSphere is a simple quiz-making space for friends, families and groups. It helps people turn the little things they know about one another into a light-hearted challenge, or use playful questions to explore different personality styles.

What you can make

Friendship quizzes

Create a “How well do you know me?” quiz using your own questions or the built-in question library. Add four answer choices and select the correct answer for each question. When someone completes a new quiz link, their score is calculated using the saved answer key.

Personality quizzes

Write prompts with four possible answers and assign each answer to one of your result types. AskSphere counts the selected result types and displays the most frequent one. If there is a tie, the first result type in your list is selected.

Starter templates

Choose from ready-made friendship and personality starters, then personalize the title, questions and answers. Templates are starting points, not fixed tests: edit or remove anything that does not fit your group.

How to make a thoughtful quiz

  1. Keep questions specific. Ask about favourite foods, shared memories, travel styles or harmless preferences rather than vague questions.
  2. Make answer choices distinct. Four choices should be easy to understand and should not overlap too much.
  3. Respect privacy. Avoid passwords, contact details, health information, secrets or anything another person would not want shared.
  4. Check your quiz before sharing. Preview the wording and confirm every friendship answer key or personality outcome assignment.
  5. Share with the right people. Anyone who receives a quiz link may be able to open it, so treat links as shareable content.

Scores and privacy

For new friendship quiz links, correct answers are stored server-side and are not included in the public quiz response. Individual answers are processed to calculate a result and are not saved as a response history. A player's nickname and score are added to the public leaderboard only when that player explicitly opts in. Do not use your real name if you do not want it displayed.

AskSphere currently has no user accounts and does not offer self-service quiz or score deletion. Review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before using the service. For questions or data requests, use the Contact page; a monitored support address still needs to be configured by the site operator.

Our approach

AskSphere is designed for entertainment and friendly connection. Quiz outcomes are playful, not scientific personality assessments or professional advice. Be kind, keep questions comfortable, and let people skip anything they do not want to answer.
